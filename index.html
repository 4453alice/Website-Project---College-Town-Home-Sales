Every university pulls on the homes around it. Some pull hard, and homes near the quad cost far more per square foot. Some barely tug. One seems to push. Pick a campus, then open a card.

SIX WAYS TO READ THE PULL

(01) GRAVITY FIELD

The gravity field

Each dot is one sale. Darker dots cost more per square foot. Rings mark 1, 2, 4 and 8 miles from the campus landmark. Click a dot for its story.

(02) GRAVITY CURVE

The gravity curve

Median price per square foot by distance from campus. The selected campus is in slate blue, Illinois in burnt orange for reference, every other campus in gray. Hover a point for details.

SHOW AS TABLE

(03) WHO PULLS HARDEST

Who pulls hardest

Campus premium compares the median price per sq ft within 1 mile of campus with homes 4 or more miles away. +50% means close-in homes cost half again as much per square foot.

(04) YOUR BUDGET

What your budget buys

Set a budget and a minimum number of bedrooms. For each campus we look at the homes that actually sold within your budget and describe a typical one.

(05) HOW FAST

How fast homes go

Median days on market, from listing to accepted offer, for sales that have both dates. A shorter line means a hotter market.
