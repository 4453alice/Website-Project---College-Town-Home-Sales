campus gravity · a field study of 35,371 home sales

every campus pulls on the homes around it

Some pull hard, and homes near the quad cost far more per square foot. Some barely tug. One seems to push. Choose a circle to visit a campus.

the gravity field

Each dot is one sale. Darker dots cost more per square foot. Rings mark 1, 2, 4 and 8 miles from the campus landmark. Click a dot for its story.

Median price per square foot by distance from campus. The selected campus is in blue, Illinois in terracotta for reference, every other campus in sand. Hover a point for details.

who pulls hardest

Campus premium compares the median price per sq ft within 1 mile of campus with homes 4 or more miles away. +50% means close-in homes cost half again as much per square foot.

Median days on market, from listing to accepted offer, for sales that have both dates. A shorter line means a hotter market.
