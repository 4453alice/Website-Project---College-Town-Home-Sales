Campus Gravity

Every university pulls on the homes around it. Some pull hard: prices per square foot climb steeply as you get closer to the quad. Others barely tug at all, and one seems to push. Pick a campus and feel its pull.

01 The gravity field

Each dot is one sale. Brighter dots cost more per square foot. Rings mark 1, 2, 4 and 8 miles from the campus landmark.

02 The gravity curve

Median price per square foot by distance from campus. The selected campus is in blue, Illinois in orange for reference, all others in gray. Hover a point for details.

03 Gravity leaderboard

Campus premium = median price per sq ft within 1 mile of campus, compared with homes 4 or more miles away. A premium of +50% means close-in homes cost half again as much per square foot.

04 What can your budget buy?

Set a budget and a minimum number of bedrooms. For each campus we look at the homes that actually sold within your budget and show what a typical one looked like.

05 How fast do homes go?

Median days on market, from listing to accepted offer, for sales that have both dates. Shorter bars mean a hotter market.
